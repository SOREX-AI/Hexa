Hexa Privacy Policy

Hexa stores its configuration, authentication state, conversation history, skills, plugins, and runtime databases locally in the .hexashell folder in your user home directory.

Hexa does not add its own advertising or analytics SDK. Requests sent to a selected hosted model provider are subject to that provider's terms and privacy practices. Requests sent to a local provider such as Ollama or LM Studio remain subject to the configuration and networking behavior of that local software.

Hexa may execute commands, read files, and modify files when you authorize those operations through the selected permission profile. Full-access mode can access files and networks without individual approval and should only be enabled when you understand the risk.

Plugins, MCP servers, web search, browser tools, and other integrations can communicate with third-party services. Review their permissions and policies before enabling them.

By selecting Agree, you acknowledge this privacy policy and consent to Hexa storing the local application data needed to operate. Select Disagree to cancel installation.

Upstream open-source components retain their respective licenses and notices, which are included with the application.
